A Culinary Journey

In my two weeks in Colombia, I was introduced to a wide range of food that were new to me. These foods were simple yet exotic and delicious. Some of the foods didn't make it on the website because I was too busy enjoying them to the extent that I forgot to take a picture.

The food you will see here are a few that I remembered to take pictures of while I was enjoying it. Once in a while I crave these foods.
